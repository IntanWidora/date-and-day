Days of the Week (Nama-Nama Hari)

In English, there are 7 days in a week. Days always start with a CAPITAL LETTER!

Day 1 Monday
Day 2 Tuesday
Day 3 Wednesday
Day 4 Thursday
Day 5 Friday
Weekend Saturday
Weekend Sunday

💡 Useful Time Expressions & Prepositions

  • We use preposition "ON" before days: on Monday, on Sunday, on weekdays.
  • Today = hari ini | Tomorrow = besok | Yesterday = kemarin
  • Example: If today is Wednesday, yesterday was Tuesday, and tomorrow will be Thursday.

Ordinal Numbers (Angka Tingkat untuk Tanggal)

When writing dates in English, we use Ordinal Numbers (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th...), NOT cardinal numbers (1, 2, 3...):

1st - First
2nd - Second
3rd - Third
4th - Fourth
5th - Fifth
21st - Twenty-first
22nd - Twenty-second
23rd - Twenty-third
30th - Thirtieth
31st - Thirty-first

How to Tell Date in Different Ways

There are two main ways to write and read dates in English: American Style (Month - Date) and British Style (Date - Month).

🇺🇸 American Style (Month + Date + Year)

Written Formats:

October 15, 2026 or Oct 15th, 2026

Spoken/Read Format:

"October fifteenth, twenty twenty-six"

🇬🇧 British Style (Date + Month + Year)

Written Formats:

15th of October 2026 or 15 October 2026 / 15th October 2026

Spoken/Read Format:

"The fifteenth of October, twenty twenty-six"

📌 How to Read Years (Tahun):

• Before 2000: Split into two numbers. e.g., 1995 = nineteen ninety-five.

• 2000 - 2009: e.g., 2005 = two thousand and five.

• 2010 onwards: e.g., 2026 = twenty twenty-six or two thousand twenty-six.
